Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions

🚚 How fast is delivery?

We offer same-day delivery for orders placed before 2 PM within Accra. Next-day delivery is available for all other locations in Greater Accra.

💳 What payment methods do you accept?

We accept Mobile Money (MTN, Vodafone, AirtelTigo), bank transfer, and cash on delivery for your convenience.

🔄 Can I return or exchange products?

Yes! If you're not satisfied with the quality of any product, contact us within 24 hours of delivery for a full refund or exchange.

📦 Is there a minimum order amount?

No minimum order required! However, orders above GH₵ 200 qualify for free delivery within Accra.
