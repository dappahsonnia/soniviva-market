Our Story

SONIVIVA was born from a simple belief: every home deserves access to fresh, high-quality foodstuffs without the hassle of crowded markets and uncertain quality.

Founded by Rita Foods and Co. in Accra, Ghana, we set out to create a seamless shopping experience that bridges the gap between trusted local farms and your kitchen table.

Our name — SONIVIVA — embodies the vibrant energy of food and life. We believe that great meals start with great ingredients, and every product we offer is handpicked to meet the highest standards of freshness and quality.

Our Values

Freshness First

We partner directly with local farms and suppliers to ensure every product reaches you at peak freshness. No long supply chains — just farm-to-table goodness.

Community Impact
